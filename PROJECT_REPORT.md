# OSINT Tricolor · preliminary analytical report

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Basis:** publicly shared description of the "Ведущий специалист отдела защиты от внешних угроз" vacancy.  
**Status:** hypothesis-led planning document, not an actual investigation of third parties.

## What the job requires

Monitoring internet resources affecting company interests; potential infringement of copyright/neighboring rights and trademarks; case documentation; work with legal teams and law enforcement through authorized staff; examination of lawfully available court records.

## Working hypotheses

1. Suspected unauthorized streams and IPTV redistribution.
2. Potentially misleading brand usage and imitation websites.
3. Counterfeit equipment listings.
4. Fake payment/support pages and impersonation.
5. Relevant legally public court and administrative proceedings.

## Evidence / assessment principles

For each finding record source URL, UTC acquisition time, collector, lawful access basis, original artifact if permitted, SHA-256, processing history, confidence and reviewer. A suspicious page does **not** establish wrongdoing.

## Legal research priorities

Civil Code Part IV (copyright, neighboring rights, trademarks); 149-FZ mechanisms as applicable; 152-FZ data protection; applicable civil, administrative and criminal procedure. Legal relevance must be validated against current official texts by qualified counsel.

## Pilot acceptance metrics

- percentage of source items with attributable provenance;
- duplicate reduction;
- analyst precision on a manually labeled synthetic/consented corpus;
- share of cases requiring correction;
- turnaround time from lead to reviewed report;
- zero autonomous enforcement actions.

## Clarify with employer

Which rights are owned/licensed? Which sources are authorized? What constitutes an actionable case? Who signs off evidence, claims and law enforcement referrals? Is the scope public web only? Which platforms and data retention rules apply?
